How the two models work
Model 1: W(t) = 15.90t² + 28.74t + 46.94, with t = completion year − 2021. The calculator finds the earliest self-consistent completion year. Its 95% interval uses the OLS prediction band from the five annual observations.

Model 2: the PR inventory is fitted with a quadratic using the reported monthly inventory from Dec 2024–Nov 2025 plus the observed Apr 1, 2026 inventory of 27,454. The estimated queue at the CSC date is divided by the observed PR output rate from Dec 2024–Nov 2025: 9,215 completions / 12 ≈ 768 per month. Its 95% interval uses a delta-method propagation of uncertainty in the inventory fit and the mean monthly output.
Experimental trend models only — not an official IRCC/CBSA processing-time calculator. Security screening is not strictly FIFO and individual files can finish much earlier or later. The intervals quantify model-fit uncertainty, not a guaranteed 95% range for an individual applicant.
